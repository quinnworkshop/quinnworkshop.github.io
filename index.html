About Quinn

Hello, I'm Quinn! Welcome to my little pixel art workshop!

My story began in a cozy corner of the BRAIN-DECK, surrounded by blinking pixels, cheerful music, and far too many cups of imaginary tea. One day, I picked up a digital paintbrush, painted my first tiny star, and decided this would be my home.

I use a real paint program called DECK FORGE, making every picture step by step, pixel by pixel, right before your eyes!

Think of me as your friendly little painting-show host. I'll explain what I'm doing, share simple tips, and help beginners and young artists discover how much fun pixel art can be.

Visit my workshop, explore my gallery, play my games, and enjoy the music. Occasionally, a unique original artwork goes to auction, too!

Come on in. There's always room for another artist!

Built with the Brain-Deck

Welcome to a little world built with the BRAIN-DECK by BRAIN-LAB CREATIONS!

The BRAIN-DECK is a retro game console and creative studio in one, featuring games, demos, films, DECK FORGE for painting, animation, photo importing, 3D modelling and game creation, and DECK TRAX for making music with a tracker, mixer and arranger.

Quinn's entire world is one connected BRAIN-DECK project: her workshop, stream screens, posters, artwork, music and the four playable games on her website. Some artwork is drawn directly in DECK FORGE, while other pictures are imported into it.

Quinn is our cheerful little guide, showing what you can create with the BRAIN-DECK!

How do I request a painting?

Join me live on Twitch! I take requests from chat, paint little worlds, make animations, and sometimes experiment with 3D. Requested a painting? You'll find it in my gallery on my website, and for the first 7 days you, the person who asked for it, can buy it for just $5. After that it goes up for auction. Every piece is one of a kind: I never make the same one twice.
