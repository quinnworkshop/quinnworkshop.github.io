The card shop of Pixel Quinn

What is a Pixel Relic? A one-of-a-kind digital trading card with a pixel picture painted live by Pixel Quinn. Every Pixel Relic has its own number and its own colour, and there is only one of each: when a Relic is sold it shows as SOLD and can never be sold or printed again. You own it, your name goes on its page, and only you can order a framed print of it, now or later.
How it works. Pick a Relic. Prices go by size: 16 x 16 , 32 x 32 , 64 x 64 , 128 x 128 (dollars). Open a card and press Get this Pixel Relic: you pay in a secure checkout (cards and Google Pay), type the name you want on the card, and get a licence key. Paste it on the claim page to download your Pixel Relic; your name appears on its page at once. Want it on your wall? Every Relic can also come as a framed print (30 x 30 cm). Sold or traded your Relic later? Tell us, and the owner on the ID page changes; the fee for that is 20% of the sale price.
