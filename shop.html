Every picture Quinn makes live in DECK FORGE becomes a numbered card. One card, one serial number, one ID page that shows who owns it.

How it works. Pick a card. Prices go by size: 16 x 16 , 32 x 32 , 64 x 64 , 128 x 128 (dollars). Open a card and press Get this card: you pay in a secure checkout (cards and Google Pay), type the name you want on the card, and get a licence key. Paste it on the claim page to download your card file; your name appears on the card's ID page at once. Want it on your wall? Every card can also come as a framed print (30 x 30 cm). Sold or traded your card later? Tell us, and the owner on the ID page changes; the fee for that is 20% of the sale price.
